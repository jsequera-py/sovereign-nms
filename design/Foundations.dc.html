Semantic

State, or a value's relationship to a threshold — never decoration, and never the sole carrier of meaning: every state also has a glyph and a word, and every freshness has a dash pattern. Surface tints (the degraded banner, the root-cause node) are derived from these five and are not separate colours.

#3ec98f

#4aa8d8

Filtered is not a shade of unreachable. A device that answered and restricted the view is a different fact from a device that did not answer, and a firewall rule in this lab already produced a phantom outage. Giving it its own hue is the cheapest differentiator in the product.
