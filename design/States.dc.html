State & degradation

Four reachability states, not two. And one banner whose only job is to stop the interface from lying about how old it is.

Reachability

Glyph, word and hue together — none of the three carries the meaning alone.

ok Answered the walk. Normal operation.
unreachable No answer. SnmpError — the device may be down, or the path may be.
filtered The device is up. It answered and restricted the view — SnmpEmpty on the system walk. Not an outage.
never polled A neighbour observed through someone else's LLDP table. We have no management path to it.

Degraded banner

Fires when minutes_since_last_run > 2 × poll interval. Persistent, not dismissible, above everything.

Not a toast, not a corner icon, no dismiss control. A monitoring tool showing a green graph built from dead data is worse than showing nothing at all.
